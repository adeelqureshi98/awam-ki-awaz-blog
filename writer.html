About the Writer

Muhammad Adeel

Columnist & AnalystBloggerPodcaster — “Podcast with Adeel”Social Media ManagerContent Creator

Muhammad Adeel is a columnist and analyst from Kot Addu, Pakistan. He writes on Pakistani politics, society and the everyday questions of ordinary people, and he writes the way he talks: straight, clear and blunt. That habit gave this site its name. Khari Baat means the plain, unvarnished word.

His method is simple and it does not change. Research first, opinion after. He reads the official numbers and the actual text of a decision before he writes a line of his own view, then gives that knowledge away in words a busy reader can finish. His newspaper column Governor's Rule and the Fool's Paradise appeared on 16 October 2026, and every column here is published in Urdu and English together.

Alongside the columns he keeps this blog, where he writes in a different register: practical posts on small business and social media, drawn from his years around furniture retail, notes on the craft of writing, and the stories behind his podcast. He also works as a Social Media Manager and Content Creator, helping businesses present their work honestly online, and he works with readers and clients remotely.
